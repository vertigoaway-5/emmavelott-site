How it works

Buying direct takes about a minute, and you can be reading before the kettle boils.

I.

Choose your book

Press "Buy the ebook" and you'll go to a secure checkout run by Gumroad, a shop used by independent writers and artists all over the world. You don't need to create an account.

II.

Check your inbox

Your download link arrives by email straight after you pay, with the book in two formats, EPUB for e-readers and reading apps and PDF for printing or reading on a computer.

III.

Settle in and read

Open the EPUB on your Kindle, Kobo, iPad, iPhone or Android phone. The book is yours to keep, with no subscription and nothing to return.

Can I read it on my Kindle?

Yes. Use Amazon's free Send to Kindle service, either by emailing the EPUB to your Kindle address or by dropping it into the Send to Kindle app or webpage, and the book will appear in your Kindle library alongside everything else.

I'm in the UK. Can I still buy?

Of course. Prices are shown in US dollars and your bank or card converts them automatically, so there's nothing extra for you to do.

The email hasn't arrived. What now?

Have a peek in your spam or promotions folder first. If it still isn't there, go back to the book's Gumroad page and choose "Already bought this? Get your download link", and a fresh copy will be sent to you.

A note from Emma

You may notice my books aren't on the big online bookshops at the moment. I'm an independent author, and for now I've chosen to sell my stories directly to you, from my own little corner of the internet.

Buying this way means more of what you pay goes to the person who wrote the book, and it lets me keep my prices gentle and my stories exactly as I want them to be. It also means every reader truly counts. If you enjoy one of my books, telling a friend or mentioning it on social media is the loveliest thing you could do for me.

Thank you for being here, and for supporting independent writing.

With love, Emma
